## Concept

An editorial quinceañera invitation built around Daniela's forest portraits. It preserves the calm pacing and cinematic photography of the Maia invitation while giving Daniela an independent identity and event data.

## Art direction

- Mood: poised, intimate, romantic, editorial
- Palette: warm white, pale blush, dusty rose, muted mauve, deep rose ink
- Typography: Italiana for ceremony, Mrs Saint Delafield for Daniela's signature, Manrope for details
- Composition: alternating cinematic photography and quiet, minimal paper sections
- Photography: favor images with negative space for copy and keep Daniela's face unobstructed
- Avoid: green as a UI color, saturated backgrounds, generic princess motifs
